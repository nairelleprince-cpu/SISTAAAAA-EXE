A NOTE FROM YOUR PERSON

To my Sistaaaa,

✦ ✧ ✦

Happy birthday, girlie. 💙

It's honestly funny to think that we met because of a completely random Instagram interaction. No dramatic introduction, no grand plan, no idea that this person on the other side of a screen would eventually become someone I call family.

And yet, here we are.

You're my first online friend, and that makes this friendship a little extra special to me. We have never met in person, and we don't have to talk every single day for you to matter to me. Somehow, you still became one of those people who feels familiar in a world that can feel very unfamiliar.

I don't think friendship has to be measured by a constant stream of messages or by how many hours someone is available. Sometimes it's the quiet certainty that the connection is still there. Sometimes it's knowing that, if life gets difficult, there is somebody you would want to tell about it.

That's what you mean to me. You're not just a username I happened to meet online. You're a person I genuinely care about, a person whose happiness matters to me, and a person I'm glad I got to know.

I hope this next year brings you so many reasons to laugh until your stomach hurts, music that makes an ordinary day feel cinematic, little moments that surprise you in the best way, and people who make you feel appreciated without making you ask for it.

I hope you get to try things you've been curious about. I hope you find new things to love. I hope you get days that feel light, days that feel exciting, and days when you look around and think, "Wait, life can actually be this nice?"

And I hope you never feel that you have to become a different person to deserve good things. You deserve kindness on your ordinary days, not just when you achieve something or make everybody else happy.

I made this website because a plain message didn't feel like enough for the kind of gift I wanted to give you. So I made you a whole little universe instead. There are photos, games, secrets, colours you love, and a ridiculous amount of tiny details. It is very extra. Obviously.

You don't have to explore every corner of it in one sitting. Keep the link, come back whenever you want, replay a game, look at the photos again, or just open it when you need a little reminder that someone out here is glad you're in their life.

Thank you for being part of my world. Thank you for the friendship we found in such an unexpected way. I'm really glad that random interaction happened.

Happy birthday, my Sistaaaa. I hope the year ahead is gentle with you, generous to you, and full of things that make you feel alive. May your playlists stay immaculate, your blue aesthetic stay undefeated, and your future have plenty of room for everything you're dreaming about.

Here's to you, to the friendship we've built, and to all the conversations, laughs, updates, songs, and ordinary little moments still waiting for us.

You have your own tiny universe now. Use it irresponsibly. Explore the shiny things. Win the games. Find the secrets. Enjoy your birthday.

With so much love,
your person on the other side of the screen. 💙
